01/08/2025¶

Result: fail¶

Arrival Notes¶

  • Rather large, adult specimen.

  • Specimen came in 3 gallons of (pretty dirty) water

  • bag was lined with korean newspaper, I wonder if he was drop-shipped.

Initial diagnoses¶

  • Has some ich visible, but a manageable amount

  • Belly is definitely pinched, which makes me very nervous

Initial prognosis¶

  • While the pinched belly is pretty worrying, I'm optimistic.

Day 1¶

  • Specimen arrived via AA cargo, after ~12 hours transit.

  • After drip acclimation, he was placed in a methylene blue + formalin bath for 30 minutes

  • After bath, placed in a tank by himself with air stone + PVC + sponge filter

  • Due to visible ich, I pre-dosed the water to 2.5 ppm copper + 300 mg nitrofurazone.

  • Fed brine shrimp soaked in metro / kanaplex. He ate somewhat well, but not super aggresively.

  • this inspired confidence, as he ate in full copper + ate medicated

  • Frist night was a formalin dose

Day 2¶

  • Eating less aggressively, possibly not at all.

  • Breathing seems labored, but not aggressive.

  • The water is somewhat cloudy, so I performed a 5 gallon water change, pre-dosed with copper + sucked out as much food as I could from the bottom

Day 3¶

  • Still not eating much, but the food seems to disappear. I added nori on a clip to see if he'd go for that, but he seemed disinterested.

  • He swims around in a pattern: leave PVC towards the center, swim up a bit, turn around, swim through PVC, rotate (awkwardly) in the corner, and repeat.

Day 4¶

  • Woke up around 6 AM for a frag swap, came down to find the tang dead.

  • He was floating at the top of the tank with his mouth facing up.

  • He was alive and looking normal when Ruxin added prazipro before we went to bed ~1 AM. I unfortunately didn't look at the fish before I went upstairs, as I was focused on the swap.

Conclussions:¶

  • I'm not sure what happened, honestly.

  • I never saw any tang poops. I saw some stringy white excriment leaving him, but it was not long enough for me to think internal worms. Most likely internal flagellates.

  • Other than disease, ammonia could have played a factor. The fact that this guy died at night and was found at the surface could mean lack of O2, but I did not see unusual breathing from this guy, other than the somewhat labored breathes.

Similar fish:¶
