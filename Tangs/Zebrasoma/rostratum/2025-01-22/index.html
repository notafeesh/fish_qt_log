01/08/2025¶

Result: TBD¶

Arrival Notes¶

  • Large, adult specimen. ~6"

  • Specimen came in his own box with 4+ gallons of water

Initial diagnoses¶

  • Belly is definitely pinched, which makes me very nervous (especially after last time).

Initial prognosis¶

  • While the pinched belly is pretty worrying, I'm optimistic.

Day 1 (01/22/2025)¶

  • Specimen arrived via AA cargo

  • Drip acclimated for a little over an hour.

  • Given a methylene blue + formalin bath for 30 minutes

  • After bath, placed in a tank by himself with fluidized media, PVC, and a sponge

  • Dosed Chloroquine phosphate at 65 mg / gallon

  • Fed brine shrimp soaked in metro / kanaplex. He ate pretty well

Day 2 (01/23/2025)¶

  • Eating aggressively, looking quite good.

  • The water is somewhat cloudy.

  • Re-dosed chloroquine phosphate, 5 mg / gallon

Day 3 (01/24/2025)¶

  • Still eating really well, but not defecating.

  • out of concern, I made a thread here

  • Humble said this happens sometimes for malnurished fish, which checks out with the pinched belly. Holding back concern for now.

  • Changed water + re-dosed CP.

Day 4 (01/25/2025)¶

  • Formalin bath #2

  • Still eating very well, but is doing a bit of scratching.

  • I think Flukes is possible, but very unlikely (due to the formalin baths)

  • dosed prazipro out of an abundance of caution

Day 5 (01/26/2025)¶

  • Water is getting cloudy again, planning on a water change tomorrow.

  • Still scratching some

  • From what I have been able to find, this is a side-effect of CP. It could be that he's just itchy. Since we have been doing the formalin baths, I'm not worried about the flukes taking hold, and will not dose any more prazipro.
