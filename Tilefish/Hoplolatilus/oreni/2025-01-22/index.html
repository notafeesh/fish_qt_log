01/22/2025¶

Result¶

Arrival Notes¶

  • Specimens show very different behavior.
  • one is hiding under the airstone
  • the other is staying at the top, and refuses to come down

Initial diagnoses¶

  • I worry about a swim bladder issue on one of them, the other looks great.

Initial prognosis¶

  • Depending on the severity of the swim bladder, we should be ok.

Day 1 (1/22/2025)¶

  • Very social for day one.

  • Ate on the first day

  • Instead of Copper Power, we are using chloroquine phosphate at 65 mg / gallon

  • so far this looks great! None of the usual wrasse "I hate this" behavior

Day 2 (1/23/2025)¶

  • Specimen swimming around, eating, and looking great overall.

  • Added 5 mg / gallon (.075 g total) to account for biodegradation
