# Result: fail

# Arrival Notes / Origin / Price

 * One of the nicest gem tangs I've seen.

 * Looks great, but has a pinched belly.

# Initial diagnoses

 * Possible internal parasites, but should not be too much of an issue.

# Initial prognosis

 * Should be a straightforward QT.

# Day 1

 * Drip acclimated, then gave the fish an H2O2 bath.

 * Fed the fish, and he ate a bit of the food, but not very aggressively.

 * Dosed up copper, and noticed a couple spots on the fish, but not a concerning amount.

# Day 2

 * Woke up and checked on him. Found him laying on the floor of the tank, completely covered in spots.

 * Eyes are cloudy, and the fish looks completely morribund.

 * We created a makeshift oxygen generator with potatos + hydrogen peroxide to help him breathe.

 * A few hours after adding some O2, he can swim on his own for a brief period of time.

 * When we went to bed, he seemed like he was in a decent state. I am optimistic he will recover.

# Day 3

 * Woke up to find the tang did not make it through the night. Very disappointing, but
   I look forward to the next one, but will be starting him in full copper immediately instead of waiting
   for a few hours.


# Conclussions:

 * Given the timeline of this fish's demise, I think this guy suffered from marine velvet.

 * I was very impressed by how effective the potato oxygen was, and may use this trick in the future if
   a similar situation is encountered.
