01/08/2025¶

Result: Success¶

Initial diagnoses¶

  • Nothing obvious

Initial prognosis¶

  • Should be only as difficult as normal wrasses are

Day 1¶

  • Typical fairy wrasse behavior.

  • Ate on the first day

Day 2¶

  • Spends most of the day hiding

  • Copper tested at 2.9, so we did a partial change to reduce to 2.5 ppm.

Day 5 (1/12/2025)¶

  • 100% water change + formalin bath

  • Specimen is visibly struggling before bath

  • During the bath, the specimen does not seem to be able to swim.

  • Current from the air stone can move the fish around, and the fish does not resist being handled / moved

  • Post-bath the specimen continues to struggle

  • Laying on the floor upside down, not controlling balance, not eating

Day 6 (1/13/2025)¶

  • Specimen is upright in the middle of the tank, but not hiding at all.

  • Remains stationary in mucous sack

Day 7 (1/14/2025)¶

  • Specimen's behavior is actually surprisingly normal, and is eating

  • Excriment is either normal, or a white stringy substance

  • Remarkable recovery given the state this fish was in only a few days prior

Day 14¶

Customer picked up the fish a day earlier than anticipated, but we did get a full 14 days in copper.

Overall, I'm certain this fish would not have made it without QT, and I'm thrilled with the results.
