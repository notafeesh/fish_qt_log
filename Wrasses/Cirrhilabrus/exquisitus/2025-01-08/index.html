01/08/2025¶

Result: Success¶

Arrival Notes¶

  • This specimen comes from the Maldives

Initial diagnoses¶

  • Nothing obvious

Initial prognosis¶

  • Should be only as difficult as normal wrasses are

Day 1 (1/08/2025)¶

  • Typical fairy wrasse behavior.

  • Ate on the first day

Day 2 (1/09/2025)¶

  • Spending a lot of time towards the top. This is somewhat concerning.

  • Copper tested at 2.9, so we did a partial change to reduce to 2.5 ppm.

Day 5 (1/12/2025)¶

  • 100% water change + formalin bath

  • Specimen is staying towards the top of the bucket, appears to be having trouble breathing

Day 7 (1/14/2025)¶

  • Comes out whenever food is present, but still hides the rest of the day.

  • Excriment is a very thin, long white strand.

  • We are still feeding medicated, so hopefully this clears up soon.

Day 14 (1/22/2025)¶

Went home with customer after 14 days in copper. Very happy with how this one looked.
